# Wilderness International

**In wilderness lies the preservation of our world.**

Wilderness International is a charitable foundation that buys ecologically valuable, endangered old-growth forest and protects it permanently, with legal security. We work in two regions:

- 🇵🇪 **Secret Forest**, in the Peruvian Amazon (Tambopata and Manu)
- 🇨🇦 **Misty Forest**, the temperate rainforest on Canada's north-west coast (British Columbia)

This GitHub organisation is the home of our **Science team**. Here we build the code behind our long-term biodiversity monitoring: how we collect, clean, analyse and share data from our protected areas.

## 🔬 What we work on

- **Biodiversity monitoring.** Standardised, repeated surveys of birds, bats, mammals, amphibians and reptiles, and trees, to track how our forests change over time and whether our protection works.
- **Reproducible data workflows.** R pipelines that turn field records into harmonised, versioned and documented datasets, with taxonomy checked against published backbones.
- **Open science.** We follow the FAIR principles: documented data, open file formats and clear licences, so our results can be checked and reused.
- **Science communication.** Turning monitoring data into reports, dashboards and digital experiences for our supporters and the public.

## 🤝 Contributing

Our repositories are currently private and maintained by Wilderness International staff, so we do not accept outside contributions yet.

We are preparing our first **public dashboards and tools**. When they are released, this page will explain how to report issues, suggest improvements and reuse our code and data.

Researchers interested in collaborating can reach us at **kontakt@wilderness-international.org**.

## 🍿 Fun facts

- 🦇 We monitor wildlife with mist nets, point counts, transects and forest plots, and together with ETH Zurich we use drones to collect eDNA.
- 🐦 Our bird ringing records alone cover more than 270 species.
- 🎧 Our expedition podcast is called [Wildbaden](https://wilderness-international.org/de/en/wildbaden-podcast).
